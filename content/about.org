I probably fit into very few of the "typical boxes", some of my facts:
- Half Indian, Half American
- Born and raised in Chicago
- Former bodybuilder (Mens & Classic physique)
- Deleted most of my social media in 2021
- Fell in love with statistics and that changed my life
- Currently based in San Francisco

The last few years of my time has been spent doing machine learning at [[https://wafer.systems/][Wafer Systems]] -- where we have built an AI-native version of the android operating system. My work has primarily revolved around developing models for our OS and doing applied science. Some of the public bits:
- [[https://wafer.systems/blog/edm/][Evolutionary Data Making]]: frontier models as evolutionary operators to grow training data
- [[https://github.com/ckgresla/representax][representax]]: an open-source JAX library for representation learning

I would love to colaborate on creative ML projects, reach out!
