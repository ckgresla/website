Specimen

The existing dot kept as a filled shape, but its edge is eight jittered points through a spline instead of a true circle — the way the clover’s lobes are cut. Continuity with the current favicon is exact at small sizes; the hand only shows from 32 up.

Three hand-drawn frames, stepped at 8 fps — a boiling line, not a tween. The ground shadow stays put while the mark moves; that stillness is what makes the wobble read as a hand and not a glitch.

Ship

  • Header: one SVG on the 120 grid, the three frames cycled with SMIL calcMode="discrete" — no JavaScript, and currentColor keeps the dim / active / hover behaviour of the menu.
  • Favicon: frame 0, static. Browser tabs don't play SVG animation, so the boil is the header's alone.
  • Redraw until a hand you like turns up, then copy — the seed is in the file so it can be regenerated.
