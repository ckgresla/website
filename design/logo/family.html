Every form on this page is eight units of points drawn round a centre — the Pebble Mark's own recipe. The Blob is the eight fused; the Loop is the eight laid end to end as one stroke; Water is the stroke gathered into eight balls; Air and Fire are it coming apart by degrees, each particle given its element's shape, its pace and its wobble; Earth is the Pebble Mark exactly, the eight standing free. Together to apart, that is the order: Blob, Loop, Water, Air, Fire, Earth. Every switch goes by way of the Loop, so a change is always the stroke closing and opening again, point for point, never one thing crossfading into another.

Everything turns clockwise, each at its own pace — Earth and Fire redrawn a step at a time, Air gliding, Water flowing — and motion eases in after a switch rather than starting cold. The readout below weighs every form's ink against Earth's. Arrow keys move through the forms in order.

Elements

    

      
Water
Eight balls of water, the most together of the elements, each wobbling like jelly: its outline rolls through the two slowest ways a drop can oscillate, a two-lobed wobble and a three-lobed one, travelling round the ball at their own paces, each ball its own phases and depths. A swell runs round the ring every seven seconds, lifting each ball in turn and filling it a little. The boil is a whisper. Flowing: a lap in fourteen seconds.

      
Air
Loose and serene: eight wisps with room between them, heads leading and tails trailing thin, breathing so slowly you have to wait for it. A turn every sixteen seconds.

      
Fire
The stroke torn into licks, more gap than stroke: tendrils poof outward and die back at moments of their own, and every four seconds the whole ring takes a breath and flares. Jerks round in nine seconds, redrawn every frame.

      
Earth
The Pebble Mark, exactly: the eight standing free, re-rolled every frame so they tumble where they stand. Slowest and heaviest — once in eighteen seconds.

Candle
The original's lick, steady: the body fixed by the hand, no poofs, only the tip swaying and the flame's height trembling, each at its own pace. Glides round in twelve seconds. Fire's calm to the original's aggression, and the same lick under both.
